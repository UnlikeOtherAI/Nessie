Connect a Claude channel

Claude needs this channel when the conversation starts. Save the configuration below as nessie-channel.json, then run:

claude --mcp-config nessie-channel.json --dangerously-load-development-channels server:nessie

          
Claude controls its channel opt-in and permission prompts. Conversations started without the channel remain available for inspection.
